FLS Code of Conduct

Applies to Field Level Salespersons

  1. Represent honestly. Describe products and pricing accurately and never make claims that cannot be substantiated.
  2. Protect company information. Keep customer data, pricing, and internal materials confidential and use them only for authorized purposes.
  3. Follow the sales process. Log activity truthfully, honor territory assignments, and record every transaction through approved systems.
  4. Act with integrity. Do not offer or accept bribes, kickbacks, or any improper incentive to win business.
  5. Respect people. Treat customers and colleagues with courtesy and comply with all anti-harassment and equal-opportunity policies.
  6. Comply with the law. Observe all applicable local sales, tax, and consumer-protection regulations.

Distributor Code of Conduct

Applies to Independent Distributors

  1. Operate independently and lawfully. Run your business in full compliance with all licensing, tax, and import/export requirements in your market.
  2. Uphold the brand. Use only approved branding and marketing materials and never misrepresent your relationship with the company.
  3. Fair pricing & territory. Respect agreed pricing frameworks and assigned territories; do not divert or grey-market product.
  4. Anti-corruption. Do not engage in bribery, facilitation payments, or dealings with sanctioned parties.
  5. Product integrity. Store, handle, and sell only genuine product within its validity and safety guidelines.
  6. Reporting & audit. Maintain accurate records and cooperate with reasonable compliance audits and reporting requests.
